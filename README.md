# Third Spaces · ତୃତୀୟ ସ୍ଥଳ (Bhubaneswar)

> **"In a city that asks you to spend to exist, here are places where you can simply be."**

A contemplative web directory and discovery engine for zero-cost and low-cost sanctuaries across **Bhubaneswar, Odisha**—sprawling civic parks, quiet self-study libraries, and inspiring cultural courtyards.

---

## 🏛️ The Concept

Coined by urban sociologist **Ray Oldenburg** (*The Great Good Place*, 1989), the **Third Place** represents the public realm:
* **First Place**: Home (Private)
* **Second Place**: Work / College (Productive)
* **Third Place**: Community & Sanctuaries (Public connection, stillness, and conversation)

In modern cities, almost all social spaces are heavily commercialized. **Third Spaces Bhubaneswar** reclaims the civic sphere by highlighting places in Bhubaneswar where anyone can spend peaceful, reflective time without having to pay for coffee, tickets, or table time.

---

## ✨ Features

- **Categorized Discovery**: Parks & Gardens • Libraries & Study • Cultural Spaces.
- **Bhubaneswar Locality Engine**: Filter across localities including Unit 6 (Forest Park), Nayapalli, Delta Square, Baramunda, Sachivalaya Marg (Unit 5), Jayadev Vihar, Pokhariput, Unit 3, and Jhumka Dam outskirts.
- **Key Feature Tags**: Outdoor, Indoor, Open Seating, Quiet, 24/7 Access, Landscaped, Academic, Inspiring, and more.
- **Mood & Intent Engine**: Solo Reading • Quiet Reflection • Slow Conversation • Creative Flow • Digital Detox.
- **Transparent Cost Tiers**: Free (`₹0`) • Low Cost (`< ₹50`).
- **Mindful Micro-Reflections**: Every location includes a thought prompt to help visitors disconnect from screens and engage with the environment.
- **Practical Public Realities**: Mo Bus transit stops, clean washroom availability, drinking water access, shade canopy, and safety ratings.
- **Native Ambient Soundscape**: A built-in brownian noise synthesizer powered by the browser's Web Audio API.
- **Serendipity Roulette ("Surprise Me")**: Interactive breathing animation suggesting a peaceful haven in Bhubaneswar for your state of mind.
- **Community Submissions**: Submit hidden spots across Bhubaneswar.
